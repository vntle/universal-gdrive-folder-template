# Cleanup Checklist

## Weekly
- Sort files from `12_TEMP_UNSORTED`
- Rename badly named files
- Remove obvious duplicates

## Monthly
- Review downloads and screenshots
- Move finished work to the correct folder
- Archive old inactive material

## Semesterly or yearly
- Move completed courses to archive or previous study folders
- Back up important documents
- Review personal media and delete junk
- Update CV, certificates, and important records
